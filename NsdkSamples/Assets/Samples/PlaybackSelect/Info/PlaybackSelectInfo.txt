This example downloads a validation scan from Niantic Spatial Sites and plays it in place of the camera in every sample.

<b>Instructions</b>
Make sure you've been signed in with a token that can list scans.
Choose an organization, then a site.
Tap a scan to download it. Downloads keep running when you leave this screen.
Tap a downloaded scan to select it. It plays on a loop when you next open a sample from Home.
Tap "Use live camera" to go back to the camera.
