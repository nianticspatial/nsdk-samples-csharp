In this example, we'll use the Sites API to select a site to localize to with VPS.

<b>Instructions</b>
Make sure you've been signed in.
Choose an Organization where the site you're looking for is.
Choose the VPS asset you'll want to localize to and tap the <i>Localize</i> button. Each asset shows whether it is in production; the <i>Show all</i> row at the top of the list also lists the ones that are not.
Physically go to the location you've chosen and point the camera towards it.
After localization succeeds, the status panel shows the localized VPS asset and its pose, and a cube spawns at the asset's origin.
(optional) follow the arrow back to the asset's origin; it hides while you are close to and facing the asset.
(optional) enable mesh download to stream the site mesh of the localized asset; it replaces the cube at the asset's origin.
(optional) tap <i>Switch Asset</i> to move to another VPS asset of the site, or <i>Stop Localizing</i> to end the session and pick again.
